How the dates get picked

Every saved response comes back in a different person's color. Stack them all together and the days where the most colors overlap mix into the darkest days — those are the winners. The hangouts and the group tour get planned on the darkest days and the strongest week, so the most people can actually make it.

More colors on a day = darker = more of the group is free.
